MODEL ADMIN

The owner decides

The address holding the Crypto AI Model NFT is the model admin. It decides who can run the model and what it costs (free, tips or paid), the fee per inference, the fee recipient and the subscription plans. It can pause inference and list the model for sale. When the NFT changes hands, the buyer becomes the admin. Its license can be opened up later, never made stricter.

CREATOR INCOME

Paid to the admin

Inference fees, tips and subscription payments go straight to the admin's fee recipient, and follow the NFT to its new owner after a sale; the sale price goes to the seller. None of it passes through GL1F or is burned.

PROTOCOL FEES

Burned by anyone

Model creation, per-byte storage and listing fees stay in the contracts, and any address can burn them to 0x…dEaD with one public call. Nobody, including the deployer, can withdraw them.
