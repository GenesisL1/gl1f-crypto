06/ Learn

AI for crypto traders, in five minutes.

Six ideas that separate a useful Crypto AI model from a pretty chart. The studio explains each one again, right where you need it.

Features are the eyes

Signals the Crypto AI model can see at each candle: recent returns, volatility, volume surges, funding, how BTC is moving. Only past data, never the future.

The label is the question

For every candle: did price hit your target before your stop, within your window? 1 or 0. The Crypto AI model learns to estimate that probability.

Trees vote together

Hundreds of small decision trees each fix the mistakes of the ones before. Together they form a probability, not a promise.

Overfitting is the enemy

A Crypto AI model that memorised last month looks brilliant on last month and useless tomorrow. Early stopping and simple settings fight it.

Test on the future

Walk-forward validation trains on the past and tests on what came next. Shuffling time together flatters the Crypto AI model and fools you.

70% means wrong 3 in 10

Probabilities are frequencies, not certainties. After fees, a small edge can vanish entirely. The backtest tells you whether it did.
