== Disasters & conflict (10) ==

Largest earthquake, last 7 days (event_quake_max_7d): Highest magnitude of M6+ earthquakes worldwide, 0 if none (USGS). History from 1973. [live public data: USGS]
Major earthquakes (M7+), last 30 days (event_quakes_m7_30d): Count worldwide (USGS). History from 1973. [live public data: USGS]
Active hurricanes, typhoons and cyclones (event_storms_active): Tropical storms tracked the day before (NASA EONET). History from 2000. [live public data: NASA EONET]
Strongest active storm (wind) (event_storm_wind_max): Highest wind reported the day before among active tropical storms, in knots; 0 if none (NASA EONET). History from 2000. [live public data: NASA EONET]
Major hurricanes and typhoons (event_major_storms_active): Tropical storms with winds of 96 knots or more the day before: category 3 or stronger (NASA EONET). History from 2000. [live public data: NASA EONET]
New tropical storms, last 7 days (event_new_storms_7d): Tropical storms first tracked in the last 7 days (NASA EONET). History from 2000. [live public data: NASA EONET]
War attention (event_war_attention): Wikipedia views of War, World War III and Nuclear warfare the day before, versus their 90-day average. History from 2015. [live public data: Wikipedia]
Days since a major war escalation (event_days_since_war_escalation): Days since the latest major escalation in the built-in list, capped at 365. History from 2014. [computed from the candle time]
Week after a major war escalation (event_war_escalation_week): 1 during the 7 days after a major escalation (Ukraine, Israel and Gaza, Iran, India and Pakistan). History from 2014. [computed from the candle time]

Sources (everything loads in the browser; nothing to install)
- Exchange candles: Binance, Coinbase and Hyperliquid.
- Computed from the candle time: US market holidays (NYSE rules), Fed decision dates, elections, Asian holidays, expiries; Moon and planets from JPL approximate orbital elements and a low-precision lunar theory.
- Live public data, fetched when a dataset is built: DBnomics (Federal Reserve H.15 Treasury yields and fed funds, BLS unemployment); DataHub on GitHub (EIA oil and natural gas, BLS CPI, Federal Reserve H.10 exchange rates for the dollar index, Cboe VIX, Robert Shiller's S&P 500, gold); USGS earthquakes; NASA EONET tropical storms; Wikipedia pageviews (from July 2015).
- If a live source cannot be reached, the studio uses the copy the GL1F Crypto site publishes at data/world.json.
- Daily series count from 2 days after their date; monthly CPI from 45 days, unemployment, S&P 500 and gold from 40 days after the month; events from the day before.
